# MS Word Practical Assignment: Creating a Structured Document

## Assignment Title

**Create a Professional Document Using MS Word Styles and Layout Features**

## Objective

Create a **4–5 page professionally formatted document** on the topic **“Climate Change and Its Impact”** using Microsoft Word.

The purpose of this assignment is to practice creating a well-structured document using **Styles, Cover Page, Page Breaks, Table of Contents, Images, and Page Numbers**.

---

## Topic

### **Climate Change and Its Impact**

Your document should explain what climate change is, its causes, effects, and possible solutions.

---

## Document Requirements

### 1. Cover Page

Create an attractive **Cover Page** containing:

* Document title: **Climate Change and Its Impact**
* Student Name
* Course/Class
* Date
* Institution/Training Centre Name

Use an appropriate built-in **Cover Page** design from MS Word.

---

### 2. Document Structure

The document should contain approximately **4–5 pages of content**, excluding the cover page if required.

Use appropriate **Heading Styles** to organize the content.

#### Chapter 1: Introduction to Climate Change

Use **Heading 1** for the chapter heading.

Include sections such as:

* **1.1 What is Climate Change?**
* **1.2 Difference Between Weather and Climate**
* **1.3 Why Climate Change Matters**

Use **Heading 2** for section headings.

#### Chapter 2: Causes of Climate Change

Include sections such as:

* **2.1 Greenhouse Gases**
* **2.2 Burning of Fossil Fuels**
* **2.3 Deforestation**
* **2.4 Industrial Activities**

#### Chapter 3: Effects of Climate Change

Include sections such as:

* **3.1 Rising Temperatures**
* **3.2 Melting Glaciers and Rising Sea Levels**
* **3.3 Extreme Weather Events**
* **3.4 Impact on Wildlife and Ecosystems**

#### Chapter 4: Solutions and Prevention

Include sections such as:

* **4.1 Renewable Energy**
* **4.2 Energy Conservation**
* **4.3 Afforestation**
* **4.4 Individual Actions**

#### Chapter 5: Conclusion

Write a short conclusion summarizing the importance of addressing climate change.

---

## 3. Apply Styles

Use MS Word's built-in Styles appropriately.

| Content             | Required Style |
| ------------------- | -------------- |
| Main Document Title | **Title**      |
| Chapter Headings    | **Heading 1**  |
| Section Headings    | **Heading 2**  |
| Normal paragraphs   | **Normal**     |

Do not manually format every heading separately. Use the appropriate **Styles**.

---

## 4. Table of Contents

Insert an automatic **Table of Contents** after the Cover Page.

The Table of Contents should automatically display:

* Chapter headings
* Section headings
* Corresponding page numbers

**Important:** Do not type the Table of Contents manually.

---

## 5. Page Breaks

Use **Page Break** appropriately to organize the document.

For example:

* Cover Page → Page Break
* Table of Contents → Page Break
* Each major chapter may begin on a new page where appropriate.

Do **not** create new pages by repeatedly pressing the **Enter** key.

---

## 6. Insert Images

Insert at least **3 relevant images** related to climate change.

Examples:

* Greenhouse effect
* Melting glaciers
* Renewable energy
* Forests or deforestation
* Extreme weather

Images should:

* Be relevant to the surrounding content.
* Be properly positioned.
* Have appropriate text wrapping.
* Not cover or overlap the text.

At least **one image should have a caption**.

---

## 7. Page Numbers

Insert **page numbers in the Footer**.

Requirements:

* Page numbers should appear consistently throughout the document.
* The Cover Page should preferably not display a page number.
* Use the appropriate Header & Footer options to achieve this.

---

## 8. General Formatting

Maintain a professional appearance throughout the document.

Use:

* Consistent font styles and sizes
* Proper paragraph alignment
* Appropriate line spacing
* Consistent heading formatting
* Proper margins
* Consistent spacing before and after headings
* Clear page layout

Avoid excessive use of different fonts, colors, and decorative formatting.

---

## Final Checklist

Before submitting, make sure your document contains:

* [ ] A professional Cover Page
* [ ] 4–5 pages of meaningful content
* [ ] Title Style
* [ ] Heading 1 Styles
* [ ] Heading 2 Styles
* [ ] Automatic Table of Contents
* [ ] Appropriate Page Breaks
* [ ] At least 3 relevant images
* [ ] At least 1 image caption
* [ ] Page numbers in the Footer
* [ ] Proper paragraphs and spacing
* [ ] Consistent and professional formatting
* [ ] No unnecessary blank pages

Save the document using the following filename format:

**`StudentName_ClimateChange.docx`**

Submit the completed **MS Word (.docx)** file.
